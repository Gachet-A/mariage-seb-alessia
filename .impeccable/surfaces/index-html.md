Scope: index.html — the whole single-page wedding site. Mode: Persuade (guest must read practical info and submit RSVP).

Audience: French-speaking wedding guests, mostly on phones. Action: submit the RSVP form (Web3Forms). Content: original Odoo site text and images, verbatim.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The weekend is handed to guests as a contemporary Swiss house programme for a play staged in Dürrenmatt's hometown: cast of two, two acts, one castle. Refuses the centred-serif-names-over-hero-photo wedding template.

OWN-WORLD: Fir green #1e3a2b and castle sandstone #d8c4a2 own whole sections alternately; ink #141a16, ochre #c8962e only for the live/active cue (current act, focus, primary action). Archivo at condensed widths, caps, tight leading for billing and headings; Spectral for prose, italic Spectral for stage directions. Hairline 1px rules, programme folios ("p. 03"), square corners, ticket-stub perforated RSVP button.

STORY: Guest sees who/when/where instantly, learns the place and the overnight stay, reads the running order as two acts, then books their "seat" via the RSVP; FAQ answers the logistics; announcements close the programme.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: left 7 cols sandstone: masthead "Programme" + nav; ALESSIA / & / SÉBASTIEN billed in condensed caps ~clamp(4rem,12vw,11rem); bill line "29 et 30 mai 2027 · Hôtel Schloss Hünigen"; ticket-stub "Répondre" button. Right 5 cols: civil photo full height. Mobile: photo first as a tall band, billing overlapping its bottom edge, button visible.

FORM: Contemporary theatre programme, candidate 4 of 7; seed key 0b06aa6d. Raises: strict ruled running-order grid (split-flap); total palette commitment per section (screenprint); every section and FAQ answer deep-linkable (HyperCard); one held frame per act page (wuxia).

Signature interaction: "House lights": as the running order scrolls into view, each cue row lights in sequence like a stage cue sheet; the nav marks the current section with an ochre cue dot. Conditional RSVP fields unfold in place.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
